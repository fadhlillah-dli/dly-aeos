# Implementation coverage matrix

Every requirement of the master architecture (`docs/architecture/DLY_AEOS_Master_Architecture_Final.md`), traced
through four audit rounds, with its final status, evidence level and what is still open. Evidence levels follow the
master's ladder (§34; ADR-0061): E1 static, E2 controlled test, E3 runtime observation, E4 regression-protected (a
test in the CI suite on Windows, macOS arm64 and macOS Intel). Live readiness on a machine is the Readiness Doctor
(`aeos doctor`, *Kesiapan*), not this file. Nothing here claims a capability works unless a listed test, CI or the
Doctor proves it. What only the owner can do is listed, in order, in [OWNER_ACTIONS.md](OWNER_ACTIONS.md).

**How the statuses were reached.** The exhaustive audit of 2026-10-19 had 1,656 requirements. A re-audit followed
after wave 3 (513 items), then an adversarial final audit after wave 5 (221 items, each auditor checked by a
skeptic). Wave 7 then fixed or honestly stated the 114 items that audit still found buildable (#11, #12).
Each fix was reviewed by an independent reviewer, then integrated and verified, and CI ran on all three hosts.
