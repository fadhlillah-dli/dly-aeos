### Added

- **Paid routes only by escalation or overflow (ADR-0128, owner decision R34 of 2026-10-28).** A route that costs money per call (OpenRouter credits) now has its own ranking tier below every $0-marginal route (Go within its plan, free OpenRouter, loopback LOCAL) for INTERNAL and PUBLIC tasks, so a better eval score or an earlier agent preference re-orders it only among paid routes. It takes a task only when the task is INTERNAL and either two of its finished attempts on $0-marginal routes failed for quality (verifier FAIL, review findings, REJECTED acceptance; never a quota, time-out, resource, lease, exit or scope stop: ESCALATION, the paid routes are then tried first) or every $0-marginal route eligible for it is busy, out of its 5h allowance fit or cooling down (OVERFLOW). PUBLIC tasks are never paid. Otherwise the task waits (`ROUTE_CAPACITY`, or the new READY wait `PAID_NOT_ADMITTED`); the admission re-checks it for every selection path, a pinned `primary` included. Every earlier gate holds (ACTIVE decision with the owner's BUDGET decision, TaskSpec `max_cost_usd`, GLOBAL budget, data terms). The owner settings `paid_escalation` and `paid_overflow` (both on by default) turn each path off. A paid admission is never silent: the selection reason names it, the run records `caps.paid_admission`, and the lease emits the event `route.paid_admitted` and the audit record `routing.route.paid_admitted`. Two tests of the amended ADR-0124/decision C rules now run with overflow off or expect the wait. No route, policy file, worker prompt or configuration hash changes, so no ACTIVE TRIAL is voided. Bite tests with fake routes only (no spend). E2 on macOS arm64; Windows and Intel macOS NOT-PROVEN until CI.
