### Added

- **Per-role category agents with a LiveBench route preference (ADR-0126, owner decision R31 of 2026-10-28).** Seven OpenCode category agents (`agentic-coder`, `coder`, `designer`, `reasoner`, `researcher`, `writer`, `analyst`) serve the executor roles in their `allowed_scope.roles`; they are seeded disabled and enabled by the owner with `aeos work agent-enable`. They share the implementer's worker prompt, tools, limits and pool, and `opencode-implementer` itself is unchanged (it keeps `feature-implementer`). The scheduler now takes the agent from the role map at enqueue, admission and repair: a role that no worker agent serves is refused with `ROLE_UNKNOWN` and a reason that says so, and a disabled mapped agent makes the task wait with `AGENT_DISABLED` naming it; neither is ever given to the implementer by default, as every task was before. A reassign (`POST /api/tasks/{id}/reassign`) accepts only the agent that serves the task's role; any other agent is refused with `AGENT_OUT_OF_SCOPE` (`ROLE_UNKNOWN` when the role has no worker agent) instead of being accepted, audited and then overridden by the map. Each agent's `route_policy.preferred_routes` (the ordered list ADR-0124 applies to routes that are already eligible) is generated by `scripts/role_route_ranking.py` from a committed snapshot of the LiveBench 2026-10-25 (2.0) and 2026-06-25 (v1) rows, one release per category, best score first (an equal score goes to the larger monthly allowance; the noise band is kept only as `source_refs[].band` metadata and never re-orders against the score), into `backend/aeos/workforce/role_route_preferences.json` with `source_refs` per entry. Every servable OpenCode Go route of the registry, including `mimo-v2.6-flash`, `longcat-2.0`, `hy3` and `deepseek-v4.1-flash` (allowed by the owner; its data class and region still decide eligibility), is in every list: a route without a score in the category's release follows by its LiveBench v1 overall, then its allowance. Gemini 4 Argon, the Qwen family, any Claude-family model and meta-routers are never listed. `scripts/role_route_ranking.py --check` is run by a declared test (CI contract). `HARD_MAX_ACTIVE` (still 2) moved to `aeos.workforce.limits` and is now the one bound of `set_concurrency`, `aeos work concurrency` and the API body. No route, decision, budget, policy file, worker prompt or context strategy changes, so no ACTIVE route TRIAL is voided. Bite tests: a writer-role task never goes to `agentic-coder`, an unmapped role is `ROLE_UNKNOWN`, a disabled category agent is `AGENT_DISABLED` and never the implementer, a reassign outside the role is refused, agentic-coder lists kimi-k3.1-pro before glm-5.3-flash, preferred lists refuse argon/qwen/claude/meta-routers and accept DeepSeek, the data file equals the generator output, the prompt revision, policy version and context strategy stay byte-identical, and a literal concurrency bound is refused; near misses for each. E2 on macOS arm64; Windows and Intel macOS NOT-PROVEN until CI.
