### Added

- **Per-role category agents with a LiveBench route preference (ADR-0126, owner decision R31 of 2026-10-28).** Seven OpenCode category agents (`agentic-coder`, `coder`, `designer`, `reasoner`, `researcher`, `writer`, `analyst`) serve the executor roles in their `allowed_scope.roles`; they are seeded disabled and enabled by the owner with `aeos work agent-enable`. They share the implementer's worker prompt, tools, limits and pool, and `opencode-implementer` itself is unchanged (it keeps `feature-implementer`). The scheduler now takes the agent from the role map at enqueue, admission and repair: a role that no worker agent serves is refused with `ROLE_UNKNOWN` and a reason that says so, and a disabled mapped agent makes the task wait with `AGENT_DISABLED` naming it; neither is ever given to the implementer by default, as every task was before. Each agent's `route_policy.preferred_routes` (the ordered list ADR-0124 applies to routes that are already eligible) is generated by `scripts/role_route_ranking.py` from a committed snapshot of the LiveBench 2026-10-25 (2.0) and 2026-06-25 (v1) rows, one release per category with noise-band buckets (allowance breaks ties), into `backend/aeos/workforce/role_route_preferences.json` with `source_refs` per entry; Gemini 4 Argon, Qwen 3.8 Max and DeepSeek are never listed. `HARD_MAX_ACTIVE` (still 2) moved to `aeos.workforce.limits` and is now the one bound of `set_concurrency`, `aeos work concurrency` and the API body. No route, decision, budget, policy file, worker prompt or context strategy changes, so no ACTIVE route TRIAL is voided. Bite tests: a writer-role task never goes to `agentic-coder`, an unmapped role is `ROLE_UNKNOWN`, a disabled category agent is `AGENT_DISABLED` and never the implementer, preferred lists refuse argon/qwen/deepseek, the data file equals the generator output, the prompt revision, policy version and context strategy stay byte-identical, and a literal concurrency bound is refused; near misses for each. E2 on macOS arm64; Windows and Intel macOS NOT-PROVEN until CI.
